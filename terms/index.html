About Aura Moments

Aura Moments is a digital photo-frame app for Apple Vision Pro, requiring visionOS 26 or later. This website is a pre-release information site. Illustrations show an artistic preview, not a physical product, app screenshot or proof of testing on Apple Vision Pro.

Aura Moments Free

Free supports one saved and placed frame, a slideshow of up to ten photos, 5×7 and 8×10 sizes, portrait and landscape orientations, and tilt controls. Spatial-photo viewing and Make Spatial presentation are included.

Aura Moments Pro

Pro unlocks unlimited saved frames, several independently placed frames, slideshows of up to 100 photos, premium material-inspired finishes, iCloud sync and private read-only sharing. Simultaneous placement remains subject to visionOS resources.

One-time purchase

Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase, with no subscription or trial. The US price is US$19.99; regional prices vary. The localized price shown by the App Store is authoritative. Purchases, billing and refunds are handled by Apple under its applicable terms. Family Sharing is supported through Apple’s eligibility and purchase-sharing settings. If Pro access becomes unavailable, saved frames and photo copies remain preserved; features above the Free allowance become read-only or inactive until verified access returns.

Your photos and sharing

The app copies only the media you select. You are responsible for having the right to display and share that media. Private sharing gives recipients read-only access, and the owner can stop sharing. Family Sharing of a purchase does not automatically share photo content.

System behavior

The frames and finishes are digital. Desk snapping, room locking and scene restoration are provided by visionOS and can vary with the operating environment. Aura Moments does not guarantee an exact real-world anchor or uninterrupted slideshow timing.

Trademarks and independence

Apple, Apple Vision Pro, App Store, iCloud and visionOS are trademarks of Apple Inc., registered in the U.S. and other countries. Aura Moments is an independent app and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Apple Inc.
