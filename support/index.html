Getting started

Aura Moments is available on the App Store for Apple Vision Pro and requires visionOS 26 or later. Download the app, create a frame, and select a photo with the system Photos picker. The gallery shows app screenshots captured in the visionOS Simulator; artwork elsewhere is labeled Illustrative preview.

Get Aura Moments on the App Store

Importing your photos

Choose photos with Apple’s photo picker. Aura Moments receives only the items you select and keeps copies for your frames. Deleting a frame does not delete the original in Photos. If an import does not finish, check that the selected photo has downloaded from iCloud Photos and try again.

Making a frame your own

Choose a 5×7 or 8×10 frame, portrait or landscape orientation, a finish, a mat and a crop. These are digital, material-inspired finishes. Tilt can be adjusted from 5° to 25°, with a 12° default. Free includes a slideshow of up to ten photos and spatial-photo viewing. Pro expands slideshows to 100 photos.

Placing a frame on your desk

Use Place on Desk to open your frame, then use the system’s movement and placement controls. visionOS manages surface snapping, room locking and restoration. Results depend on your environment and system resources. Free may place one frame; Pro supports several independent frames.

Pro and restoring purchases

Pro is a US$19.99 one-time purchase, with no subscription or trial. Regional prices vary; the price shown in the App Store is authoritative. To restore, use Restore Purchases in the app while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase, with an internet connection. Family Sharing is supported through Apple’s purchase-sharing settings. Sharing a purchase does not automatically share your photos. If access is unavailable, your saved frames and selected photo copies are preserved. Contact Apple for purchase billing and refund requests.

iCloud sync

Pro can sync selected photos, frame names and settings through your private iCloud account. Check your iCloud sign-in, available storage and connection if changes are delayed. Allow time for large photos to upload and download.

Private sharing

Pro supports private invitations to shared frames through iCloud. Recipients have read-only access; the owner manages invitations and can stop sharing. Share only photos you have permission to share.

Still need a hand?

Email jorrian@mac.com with your visionOS version, what you expected, and what happened. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or private photos unless they are necessary and you choose to include them.
