Privacy, by design

Aura Moments has no advertising, analytics SDK, developer account system, environment scanning or third-party app backend. Your photos are not used for advertising, marketing, profiling or tracking.

Photos you choose

The app receives only photos you explicitly select with Apple’s photo picker. It copies those files into its own storage so your frames can display them. Deleting a saved frame never changes or deletes the original in Photos. Free frame data stays on your device. Onboarding preferences are also stored locally.

iCloud and private sharing

With Pro, selected photos, frame names and presentation settings can sync to your private iCloud database using Apple’s iCloud services. This information is linked to your Apple Account for app functionality. Private invitations are owner-managed, and recipients have read-only access. The owner can stop sharing at any time. Apple’s own privacy terms govern its services.

Purchases

Apple handles purchases and purchase status through the App Store. Aura Moments uses verified purchase information to determine Pro access. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Your surroundings

Aura Moments does not request detailed room-surface information or store environment geometry, hand data, head movement or frame world coordinates. visionOS manages frame movement, surface snapping, room locking and restoration.

This website

This information site is hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, tracking scripts, contact-form backend, accounts or website checkout. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and request details to provide and secure its hosting service. Its privacy statement explains those practices.

GitHub’s privacy statement

When you contact support

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it to respond to your request. Please do not include passwords or payment details.

Your choices and questions

You choose what to import and share. You can delete saved frames without deleting your Photos originals and stop sharing frames you own. For questions about this policy or information sent to support, contact jorrian@mac.com. This policy may be updated as the app changes; the effective date identifies the current version.
